49. FINAL QUALITY BAR
=====================

Before completing the design, check:

Does it look like a ₹10Cr+ SaaS/fintech product?

Does the dashboard feel premium?

Can a retailer understand the interface within 10 seconds?

Can common transactions be completed in minimal clicks?

Does the mobile version feel intentionally designed?

Are financial states extremely clear?

Are errors and processing states obvious?

Does every page have a useful empty/loading/error state?

Is the design system reusable?

Does the application feel cohesive from marketing website through admin panel?

The final result should feel like a product that could realistically be launched as a modern Indian B2B fintech/service platform in 2026.

Build the UI with production-quality spacing, typography, components, responsive behavior and realistic data.

Prioritize exceptional UX over simply filling the screen with features.
